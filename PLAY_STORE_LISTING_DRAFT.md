## Full description
StayDesk helps paying guest (PG) owners organize day-to-day property operations.

- **Tenant records:** Names, phone numbers, joining dates, room assignments, emergency contacts and limited ID references where necessary.
- **Room and bed allocation:** See occupied and vacant beds, assign tenants and record check-outs.
- **Rent tracking:** Record full or partial payments, monthly balances, advance deposits and payment history.
- **Receipts:** Print or save a receipt for a recorded payment.
- **Outstanding rent:** See balances by month and open a pre-filled WhatsApp reminder for review.
- **Expenses and reports:** Track operating costs, monthly collections, outstanding rent and estimated net cash flow.
- **Roommate details:** Preview roommate names and only those phone numbers tenants have agreed to share.

The current prototype stores data locally on one device and does not provide cloud synchronization or automatic messaging. Do not advertise those capabilities until implemented and tested.
